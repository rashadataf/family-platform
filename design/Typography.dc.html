Typography
Two faces and the system monospace. Newsreader sets dates and screen titles and is never used below 20px, because a serif at 13px on a phone is unreadable in daylight. Instrument Sans does everything else, and mono is reserved for machine identifiers. Thirteen steps, and a screen that needs a fourteenth is a screen with too much on it.

Rules
Measure
Body copy is capped at 62 characters. A 390px phone reaches about 48 characters of body.md inside the 24px inset, so the cap only ever binds on tablet and web — where it means a column, never the full window width.
Sizes per screen
At most three type steps in one screen region. A row with a title, a subtitle, a time and a badge is four, and one of them is a badge, not text.
Fallbacks
Newsreader falls back to Georgia, Instrument Sans to Helvetica Neue. Both are metric-close, so an export or a font failure shifts line breaks but never the layout.
Never
No italic for emphasis in UI, weight carries it. No letter-spacing on body sizes. No text below 12px, with one exception: the 11px overline and mono steps, which are short, uppercase or numeric, and never the only place a fact appears.
