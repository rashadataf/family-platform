Colour
Every colour in the product is one of these roles. A screen never names a colour, it names a role, and the role resolves per theme. This is why dark mode costs nothing later: no component knows which theme it is in. A raw hex in a component is a defect.

Contrast floor
text.primary and text.secondary clear 4.5:1 on canvas, raised and sunken in both themes. text.tertiary is 4.5:1 on canvas and raised, and is not permitted on sunken. Disabled labels hold 3:1. Status foregrounds are measured against their own tint, not against canvas. text.onAction also clears 4.5:1 directly against every status foreground — the pairing an avatar's initials need, since an avatar's background IS a status foreground colour, not its tint. A pairing outside this table has not been checked and is not approved.
