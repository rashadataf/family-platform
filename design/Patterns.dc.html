Patterns that are not negotiable
Four rules in the constitution have no meaning unless the interface carries them. These are not component variants to pick from — they are the required shape of any screen that touches an AI proposal, a child’s record, a reminder, or a resource identified by an id.

PATTERN 01
AI proposes, the domain decides
Constitution VII · ARCHITECTURE §5.5, §5.8
An extraction result is what a model believes, with a confidence and a citation. It is stored in the AI context, it is not a document attribute, and no reminder may be generated from it. A proposal is rendered in status.proposed, is labelled as not saved, and shows where it came from.
NEVER
Render a proposal as if it were a fact. Auto-accept anything a family would have to undo. Hide the confidence or the citation.
ALWAYS
Show the source, keep Confirm and Dismiss equally reachable, and write nothing until a person taps Confirm.

PATTERN 02
A child is a record, not an account
Constitution VI · ARCHITECTURE §5.2
Children have no credentials and no login path. Their records are reachable only through an active guardianship relationship — family membership alone is not enough. The interface has to make the gate visible rather than silently hiding rows, or a parent cannot tell what the other guardian can see.
NEVER
Show a child’s record to a non-guardian family member. Offer a “create account for this child” affordance anywhere.
ALWAYS
Mark gated surfaces with sensitive tokens and the lock, say who may see it, and state that reads are logged.

PATTERN 03
Why did the app tell me this
ARCHITECTURE §5.6
Every scheduled reminder carries the rule id, the rule version and the source aggregate that triggered it. That is stored precisely so the question always has an answer, which means the answer belongs on the reminder itself, not buried in a settings screen.
NEVER
A notification with no traceable origin. A reminder a family cannot turn off at the rule that created it.
ALWAYS
One provenance line on every reminder: which rule, which version, which document or event.

PATTERN 04
Someone else’s data does not exist
Constitution V
A cross-family access attempt returns not-found, never forbidden, so that the existence of a resource is never disclosed. The audit log records the real reason. This is a UI obligation as much as an API one: the screen must not leak through wording, through a differently-shaped error, or through timing.
NEVER
“You do not have permission to view this document.” That sentence confirms the document exists.
ALWAYS
The same not-found screen a genuinely missing resource would produce, with the same wording and the same actions.
