Not now
Stacked, never side by side. Two 50% buttons inside the 24px insets give each a 165px target with a 50/50 chance of the wrong one under a thumb, and the destructive one is always the one you hit.
12px between them, space.3. The escape is a ghost so the eye lands on the primary first, and it says what it does — “Not now”, not “Cancel”.
Rules
One primary
A screen with two primary buttons has not decided what it is for. Demote one to secondary.
Labels are verbs
“Add document”, “Confirm expiry date”. Never “OK”, never “Submit”, never a noun on its own.
Disabled explains itself
A disabled primary always has helper text saying what is missing. A dead button with no reason is a dead end.
Destructive confirms
Destructive never acts on tap. It opens a dialog that names the thing being destroyed and what cannot be undone.
